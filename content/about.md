The **DIY Home Computing Club** is a student organization at Colorado State
University for anyone curious about how computers and networks actually work.
We cover general tech, home computing, and homelabbing — from building your first
PC to running a rack of servers in your basement.

## What we do

- Hands-on workshops: building, repairing, and upgrading hardware
- Homelab show-and-tell: self-hosting, virtualization, networking, storage
- Operating systems, the command line, and automation
- Group projects and help sessions
